# Brisbane Jet Lag: materials checklist

Two teams. Quantities below are per team unless it says otherwise.

## Pack into each team's backpack

- [ ] 10 red plastic cups (Win at Sideshow Alley, Bowen Hills). Pack 12 so a crushed cup doesn't end the challenge.
- [ ] 1 coin for flipping (Get Lucky, Victoria Park). Any ordinary Australian coin. A 20c or 50c is easiest to flip and find again.
- [ ] 1 blindfold (Count the Nerds, Milton/Auchenflower). A sleep mask or a scarf.
- [ ] 1 pen (Solve a Sudoku, The Valley). The challenge says to acquire one, so this is optional, but a spare avoids a wasted trip.
- [ ] Cash float in notes: one $20, one $10 and one $5 (Gather Exact Change, West End). The team only uses the note that matches the tier they lock in. It must be notes, since coins they already hold don't count.
- [ ] A spare cup or a few napkins for draining and counting pearls (Count the Boba, South Brisbane).

## Each team must have

- [ ] At least one iPhone, for the Measure app (Sideshow Alley and Make Your Mark). If a team is all Android, give them a 5 metre tape measure or a pre-measured length of string instead.
- [ ] A phone with plenty of free storage and battery. Film a Bird needs up to 15 minutes of continuous video.
- [ ] A power bank and cable. Five hours of GPS, video and the game site will flatten a phone.
- [ ] A random number generator and a list randomiser. Any free app or website works (Count the Nerds, Timed Hide and Seek, Order from a Convenience Store).
- [ ] A timer or stopwatch app (Predict Business, Timed Hide and Seek, Film a Bird).
- [ ] Something to prop a phone up hands-free, for the 3 point tier of Film a Bird. A small phone tripod, or just the backpack.
- [ ] A go card or contactless bank card for public transport.
- [ ] Water, hat, sunscreen, comfortable shoes.

## Spending money per team

Purchases the challenges require:

| Challenge | What they buy | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|
| Count the Boba | One bubble tea with pearls | $8 to $10 |
| Solve a Sudoku | A newspaper or puzzle book | $3 to $8 |
| Count the Nerds | A box of Nerds | $2 to $4 |
| Gather Exact Change | Spends part of the cash float | up to $20 |
| Make Your Mark | Optional item to kick | $0 to $15 |
| Public transport | Roughly 8 to 12 trips | $5 to $10 |

Suggested budget: **about $70 per team**, made up of the $35 note float plus about $35 for purchases and transport. Food and drinks for the players themselves are extra.

Everything except Gather Exact Change can be paid by card.

## Organiser to arrange

- [ ] Charlie needs to be home and someone needs to be there to let teams in (Tell Charlie We Say Hi). If you are out playing or running the game, line up a housemate or neighbour for the whole 5 hours.
- [ ] Reset the game in the app before the start (admin passcode is in config.js).
- [ ] Check opening hours on the day for: a bubble tea shop in South Brisbane, a newsagent or supermarket in The Valley, a petrol station in East Brisbane/Norman Park.
- [ ] Check Roma Street Parklands and the Victoria Park complex are open and not closed for an event.
- [ ] Decide whether Fake location stays switched on for the real game.
- [ ] A group chat for both teams and the admin, for rulings on the day.

## Challenges that need nothing special

Predict Business, Rank Google Reviews, Predict a Fill-Up, Timed Hide and Seek, Order from a Convenience Store and Tell Charlie We Say Hi need only a phone.
